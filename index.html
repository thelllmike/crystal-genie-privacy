Privacy Policy

Crystal Genie · Last updated: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Crystal Genie ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects your information when you use the Crystal Genie mobile app (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

DataWhen we collect itWhy
Name and email addressWhen you create an account or sign in (including Sign in with Apple)To create and manage your account and contact you about orders
Shipping details (name, phone number, address, city, postal code)When you place an order in the shopTo deliver your order; saved to your account so checkout can be prefilled next time
Purchase historyWhen you buy products or a subscriptionTo fulfil orders, provide subscription access and send order confirmations
Crystal scan history and saved crystalsWhen you identify or save a crystalTo show your recent finds and saved collection
PhotosWhen you take or choose a photo to identify a crystalSent to our server only to identify the crystal in the image
Account identifierWhen you create an accountTo link your data to your account

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, browsing history or advertising identifier, and we do not use analytics or advertising SDKs.

2. Camera and photo access

The App asks for permission to use your camera and photo library so you can photograph crystals. Photos are used only to identify crystals. You can withdraw permission at any time in your device settings.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store in-app purchases) and Stripe. Your card or payment details are handled directly by these providers and are never stored by us. We receive only confirmation of the purchase, such as a transaction ID.

4. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the App and your account
  • To identify crystals from your photos
  • To process and deliver orders and manage subscriptions
  • To send order confirmations and respond to support requests
  • To keep the App secure and prevent fraud

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help us run the App, and only as needed for that purpose:

  • Supabase – account authentication and database hosting
  • Stripe – payment processing
  • Apple – Sign in with Apple and in-app purchases

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. Order records may be kept longer where required for legal, tax or accounting purposes.

7. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the App: open Profile → Delete account. This removes your account and associated personal data, except records we must keep by law. You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing us (see below).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with access controls. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
